# Demo script

Target length 105 seconds. Use the actual recorded chain proof and keep the environment visible. The shipped recording is silent local Chromium footage from the production build, ready for the owner to narrate or rerecord after QIE deployment. Local construction inputs and lab bytes are synthetic.

| Time | Show | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–12 s | PourLedger workspace, Local Anvil chain and registry | “A column may outlast the paper trail that says which lots went into it. PourLedger connects the delivery to the structural element.” |
| 12–26 s | Connect the local test provider, create Project 1, and grant the three roles | “The supervisor designates inspectors, engineers and lab signers project by project. Each attestation is signed by its designated role.” |
| 26–42 s | Create the column using the C3 input digest. Register a lot as 100 kg | “The supplier's declaration is visible and waits for an inspector. Each lot keeps one unit.” |
| 42–58 s | Sign and confirm the inspector decision, then show accepted quantity | “This acceptance binds the lot, quantity and evidence digest to the inspector's wallet.” |
| 58–72 s | Assign 60 kg to the column and show 40 kg remaining | “The engineer can allocate only the accepted quantity. Attempts to exceed it are rejected.” |
| 72–92 s | Scan or open the column QR in a separate context. Follow the receipt and lot links | “Anyone can check the configured chain, registry and record. The passport reconstructs the relationship from successful receipts and registry state.” |
| 92–105 s | Show evidence digest verification and the current release status | “Matching bytes confirm a digest. Real-world material placement and lab validity still need independent verification. This recording uses local transactions.” |

If an approved QIE deployment and a genuine lab report are available later, replace the local segment with the actual wallet consent, verified receipt and permitted report. Omit lab results when they are unavailable. Do not reuse the local QR or local hashes as QIE evidence.
